Dalfit Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026. Dalfit is made by Denver Delorino, an individual developer.

Dalfit is a home and gym workout app. You can use it without an account, and then your workouts stay on your phone. This policy explains what Dalfit keeps, what leaves your phone and why, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • Without an account, everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • With an account, a copy is kept on Dalfit's server so you can restore it on a new phone.
  • Dalfit has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and never sells your data.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app: Profile → Delete account.

What Dalfit keeps on your phone

The answers you give when you set up the app (name, gender, goal, experience, home or gym, the equipment you have, kg or lb, and workouts per week), the routines you build, your workout history, the body measurements you log, and your settings. They are stored in the app's own storage on your phone. Like any app data, they can be included in your phone's own backups.

If you create an account

An account is optional. You can sign in with Apple or with Google. With an account, Dalfit keeps:

  • Sign-in details: your email address (with Sign in with Apple this can be Apple's private relay address) and, if Apple or Google shares it, your name. Google also shares a link to your Google profile picture, which is stored with your account and shown as your picture in the app (the image itself loads from Google). Dalfit never sees your Apple or Google password.
  • A copy of your Dalfit data: your setup answers listed above; the routines you create; your workout history (which workout, when it started and ended, the sets with reps, weights and times, and the effort rating you give it); and the body measurements you log (weight, body fat, waist, chest, arms, hips and thighs).
  • Whether you have Premium.

This is used only to back up your data and bring it back when you sign in on another phone. It is stored with Supabase, which hosts Dalfit's database in Seoul, South Korea.

When you choose Sign in with Google, a Google sign-in page opens in a secure browser window and Google handles the sign-in under its own privacy policy. Google can see that you signed in to Dalfit; Dalfit does not get access to your Google account beyond your name, email address and profile picture, and does not use your Google data for anything else, such as advertising. Sign in with Apple works the same way under Apple's privacy policy.

Premium and purchases

Premium is bought through Apple. Apple takes the payment, and Dalfit never sees your card or payment details. Dalfit uses RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random ID for your install (or your Dalfit account ID if you are signed in), your purchase history in Dalfit, and basic device details such as the app and iOS version, device model, language, App Store country and Apple's identifier for this app on your device. RevenueCat tells Dalfit's server when Premium starts or ends, and shows the developer overall subscription numbers in its dashboard. RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What Dalfit does not do

  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics software in the app.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting your data, and no sharing it with data brokers.
  • Dalfit does not read Apple Health or your location.
  • Spoken workout cues use your phone's built-in voice; Dalfit does not send that text anywhere.

Your choices

  • Use Dalfit without an account. Nothing you enter leaves your phone.
  • Sign out (Profile → Sign out). Dalfit backs up first, then removes your data from this phone. It stays in your account.
  • Reset data on this device (Profile). Removes your data from this phone only.
  • Delete your account (Profile → Delete account). Permanently deletes your account and all the data in it from Dalfit's server, and removes it from this phone.
  • Delete the app. Removes everything Dalfit stored on your phone.
  • Premium. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

If you can't use the app, or want your purchase records removed from RevenueCat, email delorinodenver@gmail.com.

How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you reset it, sign out or delete the app. Your account data stays until you delete your account. It is then removed from Dalfit's database straight away; Supabase may keep routine backups of its databases for a limited time before they are replaced.

Children

Dalfit is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
