Dalfit Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026. Dalfit is made by Denver Delorino, an individual developer.

Dalfit is a home and gym workout app. It has no accounts and no sign-in: everything you enter stays on your phone. This policy explains what Dalfit keeps, the little that leaves your phone when you use Premium, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. Dalfit has no accounts and no server that stores your data.
  • Dalfit has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and never sells your data.
  • Only Premium checks go to RevenueCat, using a random ID, not your name or email.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app: Profile → Delete my data.

What Dalfit keeps on your phone

The answers you give when you set up the app (name, gender, goal, experience, home or gym, the equipment you have, kg or lb, and workouts per week), the routines you build, your workout history, the body measurements you log, and your settings. They are stored in the app's own storage on your phone and are never sent to Dalfit. Like any app data, they can be included in your phone's own backups (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Premium and purchases

Premium is bought through Apple. Apple takes the payment, and Dalfit never sees your card or payment details. Dalfit uses RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random ID for your install, your purchase history in Dalfit, and basic device details such as the app and iOS version, device model, language, App Store country and Apple's identifier for this app on your device. It shows the developer overall subscription numbers in its dashboard. RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What Dalfit does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or cloud storage of your workouts.
  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics software in the app.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting your data, and no sharing it with data brokers.
  • Dalfit does not read Apple Health or your location.
  • Spoken workout cues use your phone's built-in voice; Dalfit does not send that text anywhere.

Your choices

  • Delete my data (Profile). Permanently removes everything Dalfit stored on your phone and starts setup again.
  • Delete the app. Also removes everything Dalfit stored on your phone.
  • Premium. Deleting your data or the app doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

To have your purchase records removed from RevenueCat, email delorinodenver@gmail.com.

How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app.

Earlier test versions

Test versions of Dalfit before 4 October 2026 offered an optional account with cloud backup. That service has been shut down and the data stored in it deleted.

Children

Dalfit is not directed at children under 13, and does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
