Questions people ask first

Do I need an account? No. Dalfit has no accounts or sign-in. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

How do I get my data on a new phone? Your Dalfit data moves with your phone's own backup: restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one.

How do I restore Premium? Profile → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel Premium? In your iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Dalfit. Apple handles billing and refunds.

How do I delete my data or start over? Profile → Delete my data. This permanently removes everything Dalfit stored on your phone. It doesn't cancel Premium; cancel that in Settings.

Reporting a bug

Email delorinodenver@gmail.com with what you did, what happened, your iPhone model and iOS version. A screenshot helps.
